Visual Engineer

Every cut starts with a read on the moment — three years behind the timeline, for creators who need it fast and clean. This is the person behind the cuts.

I'm 15 years old and I've been playing Minecraft for almost 4 years — it's the world that taught me not just how to play, but how to create.

That passion is what eventually grew into my interest in graphic design and video editing. I love seeing how much my work has improved over time, and I'm always learning new things to keep pushing it further.

Outside of creating, I'm curious by nature — I like experimenting and constantly looking for ways to improve, whether that's in design, editing, or the game itself.
